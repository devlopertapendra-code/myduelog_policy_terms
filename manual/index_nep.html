प्रयोगकर्ता निर्देशिका
My Due Log एप कसरी प्रयोग गर्ने भनेर सजिलै सिक्नुहोस् र उत्कृष्ट अनुभव प्राप्त गर्नुहोस्। यो निर्देशिका संस्करण १.६ (version 1.6) अनुसार बनाइएको हो। यदि तपाईंले पुरानो संस्करण डाउनलोड गर्नुभएको छ भने कृपया अपडेट गर्नुहोस्।

  • १ Sign Up: यदि तपाइँ पहिलो पटक एप खोल्दै हुनुहुन्छ भने तलको 'Sign Up' बटनमा क्लिक गर्नुहोस्।
  • २ Fill Details: नयाँ खाता बनाउने फारम खुल्नेछ। त्यहाँ आफ्नो पूरा नाम र इमेल ठेगाना राख्नुहोस्।
  • ३ Set Password: आफूले सम्झने नयाँ पासवर्ड राख्नुहोस् र त्यही पासवर्ड पुनः टाइप गर्नुहोस्।
  • ४ Register: प्राइभेसी पोलिसीमा टिक लगाएर 'Register' बटन थिच्नुहोस्।
  • ५ Congratulations!: खाता बनिसकेपछि ड्यासबोर्ड खुल्नेछ। पछि खोल्दा यही इमेल र पासवर्ड प्रयोग गर्नुहोस्।

यदि तपाईंले पहिले नै खाता बनाइसक्नु भएको छ भने एप खोल्न तलका चरणहरू पालना गर्नुहोस्:

  • १ Go to Login Screen: यदि तपाईं साइन अप स्क्रिनमा हुनुहुन्छ भने, त्यहाँ रहेको 'Already have an account? Login here' मा थिच्नुहोस्।
  • २ Enter Email Address: लगइन स्क्रिनमा इमेल ठेगाना राख्ने ठाउँमा आफ्नो इमेल राख्नुहोस्।
  • ३ Enter Password: पासवर्ड राख्ने ठाउँमा खाता बनाउँदा राखेको पासवर्ड राख्नुहोस्।
  • ४ Login: इमेल र पासवर्ड सही राखेपछि तल रहेको 'Login' बटन थिच्नुहोस्। लगइन भएपछि एपको ड्यासबोर्ड खुल्नेछ।
  • १ Click Forgot Password: पासवर्ड बिर्सनुभयो भने लगइन स्क्रिनमा रहेको 'Forgot Password' मा क्लिक गर्नुहोस्।
  • २ Enter Email Address: पासवर्ड रिसेट गर्ने डाइलग खुल्नेछ। त्यहाँ आफ्नो इमेल ठेगाना राख्नुहोस्।
  • ३ Click Send Button: इमेल राखिसकेपछि 'Send' बटन क्लिक गर्नुहोस्।
  • ४ Reset Password: इमेलमा पासवर्ड रिसेट गर्ने लिंक आउनेछ। त्यसमा क्लिक गरेर नयाँ पासवर्ड बनाउन सक्नुहुन्छ।

यदि तपाईं पहिलो पटक एप खोल्दै हुनुहुन्छ वा भाषा र क्यालेन्डर सेट गर्नुपर्नेछ भने तलका चरणहरू पालना गर्नुहोस्:

  • ४ Add New Member: तल रहेको प्लस (+) बटन थिच्नुहोस्। नयाँ विन्डो खुल्नेछ।

  • १ Open Menu: ड्यासबोर्डको माथि दायाँ कुनामा रहेको तीनवटा थोप्लामा क्लिक गर्नुहोस्।

  • ६ Add New Bank Account: बैंक खाता थप्न चाहनुहुन्छ भने प्लस (+) बटन थिच्नुहोस्।

User Guide
Learn easily how to use the My Due Log app and get the best experience. This user guide is made according to version 1.6. If you have downloaded an older version, please update it.

  • 1 Sign Up: If you are opening the app for the first time, click the 'Sign Up' button below.
  • 2 Fill Details: A form to create a new account will open. Enter your full name and email address there.
  • 3 Set Password: Set a new password that you can remember and type the same password again.
  • 4 Register: Tick the privacy policy and press the 'Register' button.
  • 5 Congratulations!: After the account is created, the dashboard will open. Use this same email and password when opening it later.

If you have already created an account, follow the steps below to open the app:

  • 1 Go to Login Screen: If you are on the sign up screen, press 'Already have an account? Login here' located on the screen. Then your Login Screen will appear.
  • 2 Enter Email Address: Enter your email address in the space provided for email address on the login screen.
  • 3 Enter Password: In the space provided for password, enter the password you set when creating the account.
  • 4 Login: After correctly entering the email and password, press the 'Login' button at the bottom. After pressing the login button, the app's dashboard will open successfully.
  • 1 Click Forgot Password: If you have forgotten your account password, click the 'Forgot Password' button on the login screen.
  • 2 Enter Email Address: After clicking the forgot password button, a dialog to reset the password will open. Enter your email address there.
  • 3 Click Send Button: After entering the email address, click the 'Send' button located there.
  • 4 Reset Password: After pressing the send button, a link to reset your password will come to your email. By opening your email and clicking on that link, you can create (reset) your new password. Now cancel this dialog.

If you are opening the app for the first time or if you need to set up your preferred language and calendar, follow the steps below:

  • 1 Open Menu: Click on the three dots at the top right of the dashboard.
  • 2 Go to Settings: Then click on 'Setting'.
  • 3 Change Language: On the screen that appears after clicking on setting, click on 'Change Language'.
  • 4 Choose Language: After clicking there, a dialog will appear, choose your preferred language (Nepali or English) in it. Now your app will display the language and date according to the language you have chosen.

Points to Note (Rules Regarding Dates):
If you choose the English language, the date must be written in English or A.D. when doing transactions.
If you choose the Nepali language, the date must be written in Bikram Sambat (B.S.) or Nepali when doing transactions.

If you are opening the app for the first time or if you need to set up a new member, follow the steps below:

  • 1 Open Menu: Click on the three dots at the top right of the dashboard. After clicking there, a dialog will open.
  • 2 Go to Settings: Click where it says 'Setting' there. After clicking on setting, the setting screen will open.
  • 3 Choose User Member Management: Click on 'User Member Management' on the setting screen.
    Information: This feature is intended for managing separately the data kept by different individuals and managing separately who has made how much income/expense when many people (e.g., husband and wife or other family members) use the app using the same user ID and password.
  • 4 Add New Member: Press the plus (+) button at the very bottom of the new screen that opens after clicking on member management. After pressing the plus button, a new window will appear.
  • 5 Enter Member Name: There, in the place to write the name (Enter Member Name), write the name of the person who is using the app on this mobile.
  • 6 Save Name: After writing the user's name, press the 'Save' button. After pressing the save button, that name will appear in the member list.
  • 7 Choose User and Start Work: Now click on your name seen in the member list. After clicking on this, whatever data you keep from this mobile now, will be saved safely as being kept by that same user (you).

If you are opening the app for the first time or if you need to set it up, set up the balance (total) amount you currently have. Doing this will make it easy to keep an account of how much money you currently have and how much money you have to pay. For this, follow the steps below:

  • 1 Open Menu: To set up the balance amount you have, click on the three dots at the top right of the dashboard.
  • 2 Go to Settings: Click where it says 'Setting' there. After clicking on setting, the setting screen will open.
  • 3 Choose Account Management: After clicking on setting, click where it says 'Click here to add, subtract, and adjust transaction accounts'.
  • 4 Adjust Cash Amount: There will initially be an account named 'Cash'. Click on it. After clicking on it, a dialog will appear.
  • 5 Save Balance Amount: In the dialog, in the place that says 'Opening Balance', write the total cash balance amount you currently have and press the 'Save' button.
  • 6 Add New Bank Account (If necessary): If you also want to keep the money from your bank account in the app, press the plus (+) button at the bottom right of that same screen. After pressing the plus button, an 'Add New Account' dialog will appear.
  • 7 Save by entering Bank Account Details: Here, in the place that says 'Account Name', write your bank's name, and in the place that says 'Opening Balance', write the total amount in that account. Now, to add this account, press the 'Save' button.
  • 1 Go to Payment Menu: To add a transaction, you must first add a party. Press on 'Payment/Transaction' at the bottom of the screen.
  • 2 Add New Party: If the party is not in the list, press the plus (+) button at the bottom right.
  • 3 Fill Details: In the form, you must fill out the party's name (Name Cast) and phone number. You can also add an email and relationship.
  • 4 Old Remaining Amount: If there is an amount to be received, write it directly. If it is to be given, put a minus (-) before the amount.
  • 5 Save: Press save to add the party.
  • 1 Choose Payment Menu: To keep a record of a transaction done with any party, click on 'Payment/Transaction' located in the bottom navigation bar.
  • 2 View Party List: Here you will see the list of parties you added earlier. (To add a new one, press the '+' button on the right).
  • 3 Click on Party: Click on the name of the party in the list to record a transaction with the party.
  • 4 Choose Received or Given: The screen of the previous transaction with the party will open. Now, if you received the amount, click the 'Received' (green) button, and if you gave it, click the 'Given' (red) button.
  • 5 Fill Date and Amount: After the form opens, write the transaction date and write the amount in 'Payment Amount'. If necessary, add details in 'Remarks'.
  • 6 Specify Details and Transaction Method: Select the transaction method (Here Cash is automatically selected, but if you have linked a bank account, you can choose that too).
  • 7 Choose Payment/Transaction Purpose: Here BORROW is automatically selected. If the purpose is different, click there and choose one of the following:
    BORROW: Borrowing (whose interest does not have to be returned).
    LOAN: Loan (which must be returned with interest). The monthly interest rate is mandatory to write here.
    SELLS_CREDIT: When selling goods on credit or receiving its money.
    SELLS_PAID: When selling goods in cash.
    PURCHASE_PAID: When buying goods by paying money.
    PURCHASE_CREDIT: When buying goods on credit or paying for it.
    EXPENSE: Expense (other than buying goods, like: when giving wages or salary).
    INCOME: Income (other than selling goods, like: when getting wages or salary).
  • 8 Interest and Due Date: If LOAN is chosen, the monthly interest rate is mandatory to write. If there is a due date to pay, you can put it in 'Repayment Details'.
  • 9 Save: After filling out all the necessary details, press the 'Save' button at the top. After that, your transaction will be saved in the relevant party, and you will return to the screen showing the list of transactions.
  • 1 Choose Income/Expense Menu: To keep a record of income or expenses, click on 'Income/Expense' located in the bottom navigation bar.
  • 2 Choose Income or Expense: The screen showing the list of income or expenses will open. Now, if it's income, click the 'Add Income' (green) button, and if it's an expense, click the 'Add Expense' (red) button. After clicking the button, the screen to add income or expense will appear.
  • 3 Enter Date: Here, where it says date, if the language is Nepali, write the date in Nepali, and if the language is English, write the date in English.
  • 4 Choose Category: Choose the correct category in which the income or expense was made. To select a category, click on 'Choose' and select a category from the dialog that appears there.
  • 5 Add Amount and Remarks: Where it says amount, write the amount of the income or expense. If there are any additional details, write them in Remarks.
  • 6 Choose Method: Select the medium or account in which the amount was added or reduced.
  • 7 Save: After filling out all the details, press the 'Save' button at the top.
  • 1 Open Menu: To view the full statement of the party with whom a transaction or deal was made, click on the three dots at the top right of the screen.
  • 2 Go to Reports: After clicking on the three dots, click on 'Reports' from the menu that appears there.
  • 3 Choose Party Statement: After clicking on Reports, click on 'Party Statement'. After clicking here, you will see a statement of one party.

४. बजेट नियन्त्रण प्रणाली

प्रत्येक महिना वा वर्षको लागि आफ्नो खर्चको सीमा (Budget) निर्धारण गर्न सकिन्छ। खर्च बढ्दै जाँदा बजेट कति बाँकी छ, बजेट नाघ्न लागेको छ वा नाघिसकेको छ भन्ने जानकारी तुरुन्त देखिन्छ। यसबाट Category अनुसार अलग-अलग बजेट राख्न, खर्चको प्रगति (Progress) बुझ्न र अनावश्यक खर्च नियन्त्रण गर्न सहयोग पुग्छ।

Budget Status का तीन अवस्था:

  • 🟢 SAFE (हरियो): खर्च बजेटको ८०% भन्दा कम छ। (खर्च सुरक्षित सीमाभित्र छ)
  • 🟠 WARNING (सुन्तला): खर्च बजेटको ८०% देखि १००% बीच पुगेको छ। (बजेट सकिन लागेको छ, ध्यान दिनुहोस्)
  • 🔴 CROSSED (रातो): खर्च बजेट भन्दा बढी भइसकेको छ। (बजेट नाघिसकेको छ)

५. उधारो, सापटी, ऋण र किस्ताको छुट्टाछुट्टै हिसाब (Party Ledger)

ग्राहक, सप्लायर, साथी, आफन्त वा अन्य व्यक्तिसँग भएका सबै कारोबार छुट्टाछुट्टै राख्न सकिन्छ। उधारो, सापटी, ऋण र किस्तासम्बन्धी हिसाब व्यवस्थित रूपमा राख्न र प्रत्येक भुक्तानीपछि बाँकी रकम (Balance) स्वतः अपडेट हुन्छ。

  • कसले कति पैसा दिन बाँकी छ र कसलाई कति तिर्न बाँकी छ प्रष्ट हेर्न सकिन्छ।
  • कारोबारको मिति, रकम, प्रकार, व्यक्ति वा ग्राहक, लिनुपर्ने पार्टी, दिनुपर्ने पार्टी, सेटल भइसकेको पार्टी र लेनदेन गर्ने सदस्य अनुसार फिल्टर गरी कारोबार खोज्न सकिन्छ।
  • प्रत्येक कारोबारसँग बिल, रसिद वा भौचरको फोटो जोडेर सुरक्षित राख्न सकिन्छ। पछि आवश्यक पर्दा ती फोटो बढाएर हेर्न (Zoom) वा WhatsApp, Email आदिमा Share गर्न सकिन्छ। महत्त्वपूर्ण बिल हराउने चिन्ता कम हुन्छ।
  • कुनै व्यक्तिको पार्टी स्टेटमेन्ट (Statement) हेर्न र आवश्यक पर्दा WhatsApp, Email आदिमा Share गर्न सकिन्छ।
  • ब्याजसहितको हिसाब राख्न, आउने मितिमा हुने ब्याजसहितको रकम हेर्न र किस्ता अनुसार भुक्तानीको इतिहास सुरक्षित राख्न सकिन्छ।

Welcome to My Due Log 👋

This app completely makes your traditional paper accounting digital, secure, and easy. Now, all your tasks of managing daily income, expenses, credit, borrowing, loans, installments, and budget are possible from a single app.

This is a smart financial management app built for individual users, families, shops, and small businesses. MyDueLog will become your reliable companion to change paper accounting into a secure, organized, and easy digital system.

MyDueLog helps you in this way:
  • 💰 To keep income and expenses organized.
  • 👥 To keep separate accounts for customers, suppliers, and friends.
  • 📊 To understand your financial condition through reports and charts.
  • 🎯 To control expenses by making a budget.
  • 🔔 To get reminders for remaining due amounts.
  • ☁️ To securely Backup and Sync data in the Cloud.
  • 🇳🇵 To keep records in both Nepali (BS) and English (AD) dates.

Its main features are as follows:

1. Multi-language Feature
Can be used according to your own language.
2. Offline Use
It can be easily used even without the internet (offline) and can be synced when the internet is available.
3. Detailed Management of Daily Income and Expenses

In MyDueLog, your daily income, expenses, savings, and other financial transactions can be easily recorded. It transforms the hassle of paper accounting into digital form.

  • Easy Search: Any day's, month's, or year's accounts can be easily viewed and searched by old transaction date, name, or amount when needed later.
  • Smart Record (Bill/Voucher): A photo of the bill, receipt, or voucher can be attached and kept secure with each transaction. Later, when needed, those photos can be zoomed to view or shared on WhatsApp, Email, etc. The worry of losing important bills is reduced.
  • Filter Feature: Photos can be filtered according to source (Payment/Transaction), Party, and type (IN/OUT).
  • Complete Reports and Charts: The complete details of total income, expenses, and savings can be viewed in one place. The overall financial picture is presented through various charts and graphs. An analysis of where, at what time, by which family member, and in which Category the most expense occurred can be easily obtained. And that report can be shared on WhatsApp, Email, etc.
  • Error Correction Feature: If there is a mistake in any transaction, it can be easily edited and unnecessary transactions can be deleted.

4. Budget Control System

An expense limit (Budget) can be set for each month or year. As expenses increase, information on how much budget is left, whether the budget is about to be exceeded, or has already been exceeded is immediately visible. ८. रिमाइंडर और नोटिफिकेशन

९. मल्टी-अकाउंट (Multi-Account) सुविधा
एक ही डिवाइस पर कई आईडी (Accounts) आसानी से चलाए जा सकते हैं।
१०. पारिवारिक पहुँच और रिपोर्टिंग
इसमें एक बेहतरीन सुविधा है कि एक ही आईडी से परिवार के सभी सदस्य अपने-अपने डिवाइस पर इसे चला सकते हैं, और सभी के रिकॉर्ड तथा रिपोर्ट अलग-अलग या एक साथ देखे जा सकते हैं।

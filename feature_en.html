Welcome to My Due Log 👋

This app completely makes your traditional paper accounting digital, secure, and easy. Now, all your tasks of managing daily income, expenses, credit, borrowing, loans, installments, and budget are possible from a single app.

This is a smart financial management app built for individual users, families, shops, and small businesses. MyDueLog will become your reliable companion to change paper accounting into a secure, organized, and easy digital system.

MyDueLog helps you in this way:
  • 💰 To keep income and expenses organized.
  • 👥 To keep separate accounts for customers, suppliers, and friends.
  • 📊 To understand your financial condition through reports and charts.
  • 🎯 To control expenses by making a budget.
  • 🔔 To get reminders for remaining due amounts.
  • ☁️ To securely Backup and Sync data in the Cloud.
  • 🇳🇵 To keep records in both Nepali (BS) and English (AD) dates.

3. Detailed Management of Daily Income and Expenses

In MyDueLog, your daily income, expenses, savings, and other financial transactions can be easily recorded. It transforms the hassle of paper accounting into digital form.

  • Easy Search: Any day's, month's, or year's accounts can be easily viewed and searched by old transaction date, name, or amount when needed later.
  • Smart Record (Bill/Voucher): A photo of the bill, receipt, or voucher can be attached and kept secure with each transaction. Later, when needed, those photos can be zoomed to view or shared on WhatsApp, Email, etc. The worry of losing important bills is reduced.
  • Filter Feature: Photos can be filtered according to source (Payment/Transaction), Party, and type (IN/OUT).
  • Complete Reports and Charts: The complete details of total income, expenses, and savings can be viewed in one place. The overall financial picture is presented through various charts and graphs. An analysis of where, at what time, by which family member, and in which Category the most expense occurred can be easily obtained. And that report can be shared on WhatsApp, Email, etc.
  • Error Correction Feature: If there is a mistake in any transaction, it can be easily edited and unnecessary transactions can be deleted.

4. Budget Control System

An expense limit (Budget) can be set for each month or year. As expenses increase, information on how much budget is left, whether the budget is about to be exceeded, or has already been exceeded is immediately visible. This helps in keeping separate budgets according to Category, understanding the progress of expenses, and controlling unnecessary expenses.

Three states of Budget Status:

  • 🟢 SAFE (Green): Expense is less than 80% of the budget. (Expense is within the safe limit)
  • 🟠 WARNING (Orange): Expense has reached between 80% and 100% of the budget. (Budget is about to run out, please pay attention)
  • 🔴 CROSSED (Red): Expense has exceeded the budget. (Budget has been crossed)

5. Separate Accounting for Credit, Borrowing, Loan, and Installments (Party Ledger)

All transactions with customers, suppliers, friends, relatives, or other persons can be kept separately. Accounts related to credit, borrowing, loans, and installments are kept systematically and the remaining amount (Balance) is automatically updated after each payment.

  • It can be clearly seen who has to pay how much money to you and how much you have to pay to whom.
  • Transactions can be searched by filtering according to transaction date, amount, type, person or customer, receivable party, payable party, settled party, and the member making the transaction.
  • A photo of the bill, receipt, or voucher can be attached and kept secure with each transaction. Later, when needed, those photos can be zoomed to view or shared on WhatsApp, Email, etc. The worry of losing important bills is reduced.
  • The Party Statement of any person can be viewed and shared on WhatsApp, Email, etc. when necessary.
  • Accounts can be kept with interest, the amount with interest on upcoming dates can be viewed, and the payment history according to installments can be kept secure.

Your accounting is important. Therefore, MyDueLog securely Backups and Syncs data via Firebase. Due to this, data is automatically recovered after logging into a new mobile. Example: Even if the mobile breaks down, your years of accounting and photos remain safe.
